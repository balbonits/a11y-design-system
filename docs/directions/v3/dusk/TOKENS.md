# E "dusk", Soft Dusk: token sheet

Apricot paper, plum type, coral glow. Flowing curves, pills and generous radii, with sections that melt into each other through curved dividers instead of hard edges. Dark mode is a deep aubergine night with apricot accents.

Fonts: Young Serif (display) and Nunito Sans (body, humanist sans). References: Headspace, Calm, Lottie Files.

Live swatches for the current mode are in the prototype's [Tokens screen](index.html#tokens). Contrast results are in [CONTRAST.md](CONTRAST.md) (0 fails in light and dark).

Motion: slow easing (`--ease-standard`), a scroll-driven hue shift on the decorative dusk gradient (`--hue-shift`, up to 40 degrees over the page), and gentle parallax on decorative curves (`--parallax-scale`). Nothing moves on its own, so no Pause control is needed (WCAG 2.2.2). Text never sits on the gradient; it sits on solid `--surface` or `--color-bg`. With reduced motion, durations, hue shift and parallax are all 0.
