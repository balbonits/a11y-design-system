# D "kinetic", Kinetic Swiss: token sheet

Warm white paper, cobalt type and rules, one signal red for action. An asymmetric 12-column grid with lots of air and no cards: content is separated by hairline rules and whitespace. Dark mode is a cobalt night with warm white type.

Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque (variable display, 300 to 800), Instrument Sans (body), IBM Plex Mono (labels and data). References: Pentagram, Collins, Locomotive.

Live swatches for the current mode are in the prototype's [Tokens screen](index.html#tokens). Contrast results are in [CONTRAST.md](CONTRAST.md) (0 fails in light and dark).
